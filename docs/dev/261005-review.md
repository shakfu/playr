## Summary

The audio callbacks of all three crates take no locks and allocate nothing; replaced buffers go back to the UI thread to be freed, and non-finite settings are rejected. All tests pass:

| crate | tests |
|-|-|

The problems are DSP edge cases, a few app-layer state machines, and test gaps.

## Items

### Fix first: user-visible, small fixes

1. **[done]** **Tape save stuck after an aborted snapshot (verified).** `crates/playr-app/src/tape.rs:373`, `Deck::poll`, handles only `Returned::Snapshot`. `Returned::Aborted` is dropped, `save_to` stays `Some`, and every later Save is refused with `AlreadySaving` (`tape.rs:559`) until the next load.

   - Trigger: Reset, or dragging the write window, during the copy, about 1 s for a 10 s loop. A device lost mid-save does it too.

   - Fix: clear `save_to` on `Aborted` and report it, or resend the snapshot. Test it.

2. **[done]** **DJ master compresses from -6 dBFS (verified).** `crates/playr-dj/src/mixer.rs:571-579` runs `playr_dsp::clip` on the main and cue buses before `vol`. `clip` passes only `|x| <= 0.5` unchanged.

   - One deck at unity: a 1.0 peak comes out at 0.88, and 0.8 at 0.77, with distortion. Turning the master down does not help.

   - `tests/mixer.rs:73` passes only because it uses 0.4-amplitude noise.

   - Fix: move the knee near 1.0, or apply about -6 dB before the clip. Add a full-scale transparency test.

3. **[done]** **DJ hot cue outside a loop is pulled back into it (verified).** `hot_cue` calls `Deck::seek` directly (`mixer.rs:344`), skipping `Mixer::seek`'s rule that ends a loop the head leaves (`mixer.rs:296-300`). `Deck::read` then jumps back one loop length per frame (`deck.rs:399-403`), and each jump restarts the declick fade.

   - Example: a 1-beat loop at 1:00, then hot cue 2 at 4:00.

   - `back_to_cue` (`deck.rs:273`) is reported to do the same (not checked).

   - Fix: apply the loop rule to every jump: `hot_cue`, `back_to_cue`, `quantize_start`.

4. **[done]** **DJ hot cues deleted on every load (verified by tracing).** `taken` (`crates/playr-app/src/dj.rs:577-581`) puts the stored cues in `loaded.hot` and sends them to the engine. On that same poll, `keep_hot_cues` (`dj.rs:524-540`) sees the engine's status still showing no cues for the new load. It writes `None` to each slot in the database, then copies the empty set into `loaded.hot`. The next poll writes the cues back.

   - Quitting in the window between those polls loses the cues.

   - `tests/dispatch.rs:1046` checks only the engine status, not the database.

   - Fix: carry the hot cues inside `Cmd::Load`, so the engine publishes them with the load.

### DSP correctness

5. **[done]** **Bypass switches write steps into the loop (verified in code; measured).** Two places switch between bypass and a non-transparent filter in one frame, so the 20 ms ramp does not help:

   - Thin: `crates/playr-looper/src/tape.rs:673-675`. Its lowest setting is a 20 Hz high-pass.

   - The voice filter at its ends: `tape.rs:104-105`. Changing the filter type is not ramped either.

   Measured: a 40 Hz sine at 0.4 with writing on, then Thin 0.001, puts a 0.15 step into the loop. The material's own largest step is 0.0021. The step stays in the buffer.
   - `docs/dev/looper-engine.md` claims "leaving 0 does not step"; that is wrong.

   - Fix: crossfade between dry and filtered signal over `SMOOTH_MS`.

6. **[done]** **Drive steps at 0 (verified).** `tape.rs:53-58` returns `x` when `d == 0`, while any `d > 0` gives at least `clip(x)`. Ramping drive from 0.01 to 0 on a sample at 1.0 steps from 0.881 to 1.0.

   - The doc's rationale is also wrong. `clip(kx)/sqrt(k)` has a small-signal gain of `sqrt(k)`, up to 4 (+12 dB), not 1.

   - The rejected `tanh(kx)/k` does have slope 1 at 0. Perhaps `tanh(kx)/tanh(k)` was meant.

   - Fix: blend dry and driven by `d` near 0, or drop the bypass. Correct the doc.

7. **[done]** **The f32 `Ramp` drifts or stalls (verified in code; measured by f32 emulation).** `crates/playr-dsp/src/lib.rs:62-64` accumulates `value += step` and snaps to the target on the last frame. The looper uses `Ramp<f32>` (`tape.rs:10`), including the rate slew of up to 10 s (`MAX_SLEW_MS`).

   - 1.0 to 1.001 over 480,000 frames: the step is below half an ulp, so the value does not move, then jumps on the last frame.

   - 1.0 to 4.0 over 480,000 frames: the last value before the snap is 3.9755, a jump of 0.0245.

   - `lag()` assumes exact accumulation, so it is off by the same error.

   - Fix: compute `start + step * i`, or `target - step * left`.

8. **[done]** **No denormal flushing (looper measured; DJ inference).**

   - Looper: with feedback 0.5 and no sends, subnormals appear at pass 106. By pass 140, 4,792 of 4,800 samples are subnormal.

   - DJ: the EQ's crossovers and all-pass decay into subnormals on a paused deck (inference).

   - On x86 these are slow, so CPU spikes are likely; not measured.

   - `crates/playr-core/src/audio/meter.rs:38` already flushes to zero for this reason.

   - Fix: zero `|x| < 1e-30` in `Lane::write` and the filter states. Add a test that runs many passes at feedback below 1.

9. **[done]** **`Svf` hazards (reported).** No caller hits these today:

   - A cutoff above Nyquist gives NaN within about 2,000 frames.

   - A cutoff of 0 freezes the state.

   - A NaN input poisons the state for good, and there is no reset.

   Both callers clamp the cutoff to `0.45 * sr` and set a 20 Hz minimum.
   - Fix: clamp inside `Svf::tune` to `(0, 0.49 * sr)`, reject non-finite values, add `reset`.

### State and ownership

10. **[done]** **Record stuck after a failed stop (reported).** `stop_recording` clears `live.recording` (`crates/playr-app/src/tape.rs:494`) before `handle.stop_recording()`, which can fail with `Full`. The recorder survives, so the next Record says "already recording".

11. **[done]** **No device recovery (reported).** After a device loss, neither the tape nor the decks reopen, and both keep reporting `playing`.

    - The DJ's queued loads never fire, and Pause commands fill the ring until `Error::Full`.

    - In the tape, `Handle::finish` waits up to `RECORD_STOP_WAIT`, 1 s, per stop, including the stop on a new load. `Drop` (`looper.rs:449`) costs the same wait.

    - On an exclusive `hw:` device, a tape reload fails with Busy: the new stream opens before the old one drops (inference).

12. **[done]** **DJ phase lock captures a wrong offset (reported).** While either deck is stopped, `lock()` sets `lock_offset` to NaN, and captures the phase error once both play (`mixer.rs:504-511`). If the leader starts after a synced follower with quantize off, the follower holds an arbitrary offset until re-synced.

    - Fix: align the leader on start against a synced, playing follower, or reset the offset to 0.

13. **[done]** **DJ cue snapped before the track start (verified).** `snap` (`mixer.rs:315-323`) has no lower bound. When the first beat falls less than half a beat into the track, a quantized cue near the start lands before frame 0, so up to half a beat of silence plays.

14. **[done]** **Tape snapshot covers more than the range (measured).** `copy` (`crates/playr-looper/src/looper.rs:251`) assumes frames outside the write window are never written. When the write window is the range, which is the app's default, the writer also writes the pre-roll and post-roll (`tape.rs:822-836`).

    - Measured: 24,320 frames differ, all outside the range.

    - Saved files are correct, because Save writes only the range.

    - Wrong: the `Returned::Snapshot` contract, the design doc, and the test, which uses `Loop::new` and so never sets a range.

### Smaller notes (reported)

- **[moved to `TODO.md`]** **Track replacement:** with strict mode off, it plays a 5 ms declick, then silence while the new track decodes and resamples, which takes seconds (inference). There is no real crossfade.

- **[moved to `TODO.md`]** **Varispeed aliasing:** 4-point Hermite with no anti-alias filter. Likely audible on bright material above about +16% (inference).

- **[moved to `TODO.md`]** **Cue routing:** the headphone level is part of the Mixer item there.

  - The cue is scaled by the master volume, and there is no separate headphone level.

  - On a PipeWire or Pulse sink advertising 4 or more channels, `cue-out 3-4` may leak the cue into the speakers through the downmix (inference).

- **[moved to `TODO.md`]** **Ring stalls:** a `Load` waiting for room in the return ring holds back every command behind it, Pause included. This affects both engines.

- **[done]** **Reset clicks:** Reset and Load while playing jump the heads with no fade (`tape.rs:887`).

- **[done]** **Minor code:** `wear_coefficient` (`tape.rs:38`) duplicates `one_pole` inline. `Filter::tick` (`playr-dj/src/fx.rs:188`) has a dead `(false, _)` arm.

## Test gaps

All added, except `clip` with NaN.

- **`playr-dsp`:** no unit tests at all. It needs:

  - `Ramp`: exact target, retarget, zero frames, `lag`, a long f32 slew;

  - `Svf`: unity band at the cutoff, `all()` magnitude, stability under a sweep, Nyquist;

  - `clip`: identity at 0.5 and below, the knee, the bound, NaN;

  - `hermite`: passes through its endpoints and is exact on quadratics.

- **DJ mixer:** full-scale input at unity.

- **Tape bypasses:** leaving Thin, or the voice filter's ends, without a step.

- **Denormals:** in the looper and the DJ EQ.

- **Tape snapshot:** with a range set.

- **Tape app state:** an aborted save in the app, and a failed stop with `Full`.

- **DJ positions:** a hot cue or cue return outside a loop, and a negative snapped cue.

- **DJ hot cues:** the stored cues right after a reload.

- **DJ phase lock:** the leader started after a playing synced follower.

- **DJ device output:** `device::build` channel mapping and `CHUNK` splitting.

- **Allocation:** nothing checks that the callback does not allocate, for example a counting global allocator around `process`.

3. Items 9-14.

4. Correct `docs/dev/looper-engine.md`: the bypass and drive claims, and the snapshot contract.
